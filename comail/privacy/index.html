Comail privacy policy

This policy covers Gmail mailboxes connected to this personal desktop installation of Comail.

What the app accesses

You sign in on Google’s website. When you grant access, Google gives the desktop app tokens to access your account. Your email address identifies the mailbox, and your Google profile supplies your sender name.

Gmail requires full mail access for IMAP and SMTP. The permission covers the whole mailbox, including the ability to permanently delete messages. Comail uses it to sync message headers, bodies, folders, and attachments, apply mailbox changes, and send messages to the recipients you choose.

Storage and optional sharing

Comail stores synced mail and its search index on your computer. It keeps Google access and refresh tokens in the operating system’s keyring. This website does not receive those tokens or your mail.

If you enable a remote AI provider for features such as summaries or reply drafting, that provider receives the prompts and message content needed for the feature. If you connect an external AI client to Comail, it can retrieve mail through the permissions you enable. The provider or client handles the data it receives under its own privacy terms.

Retention and disconnection

The local mailbox remains available for offline use while the account is connected. Removing the account in Comail’s account settings stops sync and removes its saved sign-in tokens and local mail cache. Messages stay in Gmail. Files saved separately and copies in device backups follow their own retention settings.

You can also revoke the app’s access in the third-party connections section of your Google Account. This stops further access to Gmail. To clear the local mailbox, also remove the account in Comail.

For privacy questions, use the support contact shown on Google’s consent screen for this app.
