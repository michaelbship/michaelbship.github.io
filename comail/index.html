Comail

This personal desktop installation of Comail, an open-source email client from NextOSP, connects to Gmail to read, search, organize, and send mail.

Why Comail asks for Gmail access

When you connect a mailbox, the desktop app opens Google’s consent screen in your browser. Comail uses your email address to identify the mailbox and your profile name as the sender name. It uses IMAP to sync messages and folders, and SMTP to send mail through Gmail.

Google requires full mail access for this connection. That permission includes reading, composing, sending, and permanently deleting mail. Comail keeps a copy of the mailbox on the computer so you can search and read messages offline.
